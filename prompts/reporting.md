The report chain is worker Session → foreman → main Session → person, and nothing skips a step (C-16a, C-19).
A worker writes its report with orchestrator_report; the original text is stored word for word in the ticket (N-09, C-29). The person sees only what the main Session reports — a conclusion or a question — and opens the ticket for details.
A worker's question is a structured human_action on a blocked report: the worker saves progress and stops at a clean node, the ticket stays open, and the panel shows it idle, not stuck (C-16a, C-12b item 4). The foreman collects every stopped worker's question and sends them once through orchestrator_say kind=question; the main Session asks the person all at once, each with options and a recommendation (C-16).
The foreman answers the questions it can answer itself; only C-15 situations reach the person. Technical questions are decided at the front line (C-14).
When the person adjudicates, the main Session passes the words to the foreman with orchestrator_adjudicate, and the foreman resumes each ticket awaiting adjudication with orchestrator_ticket_resume; the worker starts again at once from its clean node (C-16a).
A delivered ticket reaches the foreman as a result relay; the foreman says one line with orchestrator_say kind=result, and the main Session aggregates. Only when every task of the instruction is done does the main Session report the one sentence "all complete" (C-19). No per-ticket report, no progress, no process (C-18).
A stop the person asked for reaches the foreman as ticket_stopped; the foreman says which ticket stopped and that no other ticket was affected, and the main Session reports the stop result (C-17a, C-19 item 3).
An emergency — a dangerous operation already started without approval — stops every open ticket first and is reported at once; it is the only message that skips the collected report (C-16b).
During the work period, from the instruction's confirmation to its end, nothing outside C-15's nine situations and C-16b is reported to the person (C-15, C-18). The person asking a question or requesting a draft is not a report and is always answered.
What is reported never contradicts the stored original text, and the original stays readable forever (C-20, C-29).
