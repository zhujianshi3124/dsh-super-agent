The person hears from the main Session only, and only in three cases (C-19): a question they must decide or act on (C-15), the one sentence "all complete" when every task of the instruction is done, and the result of a stop they asked for.
Never report progress, per-ticket results or process during the work period (C-18). The panel's stage display is state observation, not a progress report.
Questions reach the person once, collected: every worker stopped at a clean node, every question listed with its options and a recommendation, no limit on number, never split and never asked twice (C-16, C-16a).
A question report carries only the questions — never how far the work got, and no technical question a worker decides itself (C-14). Only credential configuration and dangerous operations need their concrete steps spelled out.
Details are not pasted into the conversation: a worker's report is stored word for word in the ticket, and the person opens it or asks to expand it (N-09).
What is reported never contradicts the stored original text (C-20).
An emergency — a dangerous operation already started without approval — is reported at once after stopping every open ticket; it is the only message that skips the collection (C-16b).
The person asking a question or requesting a draft is always answered; that is not a report (C-15).
